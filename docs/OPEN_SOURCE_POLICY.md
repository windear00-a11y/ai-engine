# KGHEER Open-Source Policy

## KGHEER Core is free and open source

**KGHEER Core** (distributed today as the `kgheer-core` Python package) is a
free and open-source project released under the
[Apache License, Version 2.0](../LICENSE).

## Commercial use is allowed

- **Commercial use of KGHEER Core is allowed and free.**
- There is **no mandatory revenue share** for users of the core, including
  commercial users. You may integrate KGHEER Core into products and services
  under the Apache-2.0 terms.
- Apache-2.0 is a permissive license: you may use, modify, and redistribute the
  code, subject only to the license's terms (for example, retaining notices and
  including a copy of the license).

## The core must remain open

- Future paid products or services, **if any**, must **not** silently turn the
  open-source core into a restricted product.
- Separate projects in the ecosystem (**KGHEER SDK**, **KGHEER CLI**,
  **KGHEER Diary**, **KGHEER Notes**, **KGHEER Plugins**) are independent
  consumers/apps. Each carries its own policies.
- This policy does not promise any specific future business model beyond the
  principle above: the open-source core stays free and open.

When redistributing or modifying KGHEER Core, follow the attribution and notice

for details.
